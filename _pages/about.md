<h1 class="visually-hidden">Hrishikesh Kamath</h1>

<div class="prose">
  <p>Hello, I'm Hrishikesh. Software engineer at <a href="https://button.xyz" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Button Labs</a> in San Francisco, with experience building full-stack applications and AI agents.</p>
  <p>I spent 5–6 years trading and investing in public markets, and from that experience I saw how AI agents could make investing much better. So I decided to try building my own startup, <a href="https://github.com/kamathhrishi/finance-agent" target="_blank" rel="noopener">StrataLens AI</a>, a platform for public market equity research that's now open source. Along the way, I also built <a href="https://themarketcast.ai" target="_blank" rel="noopener">TheMarketCast.ai</a>, a tool for tracking private fundraising activity that grew to 3,000+ weekly users and is now part of <a href="https://www.smartkarma.com/home/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Smartkarma</a>.</p>
  <p>I love building with web data and poking around in it. Lately I've been building my own distributed web crawler and search engine, with an index of 500M+ pages that stays fresh within <span class="nowrap">1–2 days</span> and runs for under $250 a month. I write about all of it on <a href="https://kamathhrishi.substack.com/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Substack</a>.</p>
  <p>Earlier on, I was a founding engineer at an Accel-backed startup in India and a core contributor to OpenMined's <a href="https://github.com/OpenMined/PySyft" target="_blank" rel="noopener">PySyft</a>, an open-source library for privacy-preserving ML. I also did my Master's in Electrical and Computer Engineering at the University of Delaware, where I worked on LLM code generation and hardware security.</p>
</div>
